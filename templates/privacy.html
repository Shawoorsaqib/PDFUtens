Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026. Learn how PDFUtens protects your privacy, files, and personal data.

1. Privacy Overview

At PDFUtens, we prioritize your privacy and data security above all else. We believe document processing should be fast, convenient, and strictly confidential. We do not sell, rent, share, or monetize your uploaded documents or personal information under any circumstances.

2. Automated File Purging

All files uploaded to PDFUtens (including PDFs, Word documents, images, and converted outputs) are processed temporarily in isolated server storage.

  • Uploaded files are strictly used for the requested conversion or editing task.
  • All temporary files are automatically deleted permanently after processing or within 5 minutes.
  • We do not create backups or retain archival copies of your document files.

3. No Content Reading or Mining

PDFUtens processes your files programmatically using automated server-side libraries. Human personnel cannot view, open, or inspect the contents of your documents. No artificial intelligence models are trained on your uploaded files or document text.

4. Cookies & Local Storage

We use browser LocalStorage strictly to store your interface theme preference (Light Mode vs. Dark Mode). PDFUtens does not use tracking cookies or persistent third-party advertising cookies.

5. Data Security Measures

All communications between your browser and PDFUtens servers are protected using SSL/TLS encryption in transit. Server storage directories are sanitized periodically to prevent unauthorized access.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns regarding this Privacy Policy or file data security, please feel free to reach out via our GitHub repository or contact our support team.
